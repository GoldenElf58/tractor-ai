Architecture Plan

main inputs:
    108 cards (108 x 10 = 1080 one-hot encoded total):
        0 - not seen
        1 - in hand
        2 - in trash (if leader)
        3 - AI played previously
        4 - partner played previously
        5 - oponent a played previously
        6 - oponent b played previously
        7 - partner played this trick
        8 - opponent a played this trick
        9 - opponent b played this trick

    game info (43 total):
        0 - 0-26+ - hand size (normalized)
        1 - 0-108 - cards remaining in draw pile (normalized)
        2-5 - one-hot - AI ID
        6-9 - one-hot - round leader
        10-13 - one-hot - trick leader
        14-19 - one-hot encoding - lead suit (5 effective suits + none)
        20 - 2-14 - dominant rank (normalized)
        21 - 2-14 - team one level (normalized)
        22 - 2-14 - team two level (normalized)
        23 - 0-200+ - offense points (normalized)
        24 - 0-200+ - defense points (normalized)
        25-27 - one-hot encoding - game phase (drawing, burying, trick-taking)
        28-32 - one-hot - suits partner followed off-suit in
        33-37 - one-hot - suits opponent a followed off-suit in
        38-42 - one-hot - suits opponent b followed off-suit in

latent space: game state in --> 64 float latent space out

suit decider extra input (0 total):
    none

suit decider output (6 total):
    suit (effective) probabilities (5 effective + mix/none)

filter by suits present; mix not allowed for trick leader (throws not supported)

structure decider extra info (6 total):
    one-hot encoding - suit (5 effective + mix/none)

structure decider output (4 total):
    0-1 - probability single
    0-1 - probabiltiy (consecutive pair)
    0-1 - probability any/mix (only applicable for following trick leader)
    int - pair length (3 == 3 pairs / 6 cards)

filter by valid structures

final card decider extra info (10 total):
    one-hot encoding - suit (5 effective + mix/none)
    one-hot encoding - structure (single, (consecutive) pair, any)
    int - quantity

final card decider output (108 total):
    0-1 x 108 - card play probabilities

model input/output summary:
    latent space - 1,123 in, 64 out
    suit decider - 64 in, 6 out
    structure decider - 70 in, 4 out
    card decider - 74 in, 108 out

handle output:
    single:
        highest legal card probability is played (within chosen suit)
    pairs:
        trick leader or following and mix not chosen:
            highest probability legal pair (sum card probabilities) within chosen suit
        following but mix chosen:
            highest probability two legal cards (always the case because mix will be filtered if
            pair must be played
    consecutive pairs:
        trick leader:
            highest probability legal consecutive pair (if multiple); determined by the sum of the
            probabilities given to all the cards in each consecutive pair
        following:
            consecutive pair:
                same as trick leader
            mixes/other:
                first select the highest probability consecutive pair(s) within the chosen suit (or
                 all that must be played)
                then select the highest probability legal pairs within the chosen suit (or all that
                 must be played)
                then select the highest probability legal cards within the chosen suit


Training Plan

Make basic bot
Generate 100,000 games for data
Train model
Once it reaches a similar win rate to basic bot, begin self-play with PPO
